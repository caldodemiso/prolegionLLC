Who We Are

Prolegion Construction LLC

At Prolegion Construction LLC, we take pride in delivering dependable, high-quality drywall and ceiling work built around the details that matter. Based in Freeport and serving Northwest Florida, we provide professional metal framing, drywall hanging and finishing, texture, painting, repairs, fence installation and repairs, demolition, fiberglass batt insulation, and acoustical ceiling grid and tile installation.

From small residential repairs to commercial build-outs, we show up prepared, communicate clearly, protect the jobsite, and work hard to leave every project clean and ready for the next phase.

We believe in honest work, fair pricing, and finishes that look right the first time—because your home, business, and reputation deserve quality you can see.
